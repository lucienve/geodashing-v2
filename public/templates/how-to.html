HOW TO PLAY

How to Play

The core premise of Geodashing is simple: travel to randomly generated geographic coordinates, known as Dashpoints, and log your visit. Unlike traditional geocaching, there is no physical object hidden at the location. The destination itself is the reward.

The Monthly Game Cycle

Geodashing operates on a monthly schedule. At the beginning of each calendar month, a brand new set of thousands of Dashpoints are algorithmically generated across the globe. Our system intentionally avoids generating points over major oceans and lakes, but beyond that, the points fall randomly across the landscape. You have exactly one month to visit any accessible points before the game board resets entirely.

How to Log a Visit

When you decide to pursue a Dashpoint, use your GPS device or smartphone to navigate as close to the dashpoint coordinates as you safely and legally can.

  1. Reach the Zone: To formally claim a Dashpoint, you must reach a location within 100 meters of the coordinates.
  2. Take a Photo: If you can, snap a picture at your closest approach. A picture is worth a thousand words, and these places are likely to be ones never seen on street view or a casual photograph.
  3. Submit Your Log: Once you establish an account and log into the site, you can upload photos and record your visit. Reading other players' comments and viewing their photos is a lot of the fun!

Scoring and The Leaderboard

Though points are awarded for being the first to log a Dashpoint, the real reward is the journey.

  • First to Log: 3 Points
  • Second to Log: 2 Points
  • Third (and subsequent): 1 Point

The Global Leaderboard tracks your accumulated score through the month, and resets at the start of the next.

Join the Community

Geodashing is a uniquely global game, and half the fun is discussing the challenges, organizing team rushes, and analyzing the dashpoints with fellow players. You can join the discussion by visiting the Dashers Google Group or by sending an email directly to dashers+subscribe@geodashing.org.

Safety & Legality

Because Dashpoints are assigned wholly at random by a computer, many will land on private property, treacherous terrain, or potentially dangerous places. You must always obey local laws and respect private property. No part of this game requires you to trespass or put yourself in physical danger. If a Dashpoint is inaccessible, simply walk away. Finding the easiest legal path to the 100-meter boundary is part of the challenge.
