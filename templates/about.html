ABOUT GEODASHING V2

The Global GPS Challenge

Welcome to this reimplementation of Geodashing. This site is dedicated to preserving the spirit of one of the earliest and most unique GPS-based games.

Geodashing was originally conceived and created in the early 2000s by an individual known in the GPS gaming community as Scout. Emerging around the same time as Geocaching, Geodashing offered a different type of challenge: one focused not on finding physical containers, but on reaching randomly generated geographical coordinates within a specific time frame.

Scout launched the original Geodashing website as a hub for players worldwide. The game quickly garnered a dedicated following due to its simplicity, the spirit of exploration it fostered, and the fairness of its randomly generated dashpoints.

For many years, Geodashing operated on a monthly cycle. Every month, thousands of new coordinates (known as "dashpoints") were generated globally. Players would use their GPS receivers to navigate as close as possible to these points. Points were awarded based on how close a player could get, encouraging exploration of potentially obscure or difficult-to-reach locations.

Scout maintained the original site and community with passion and dedication for well over a decade, providing a platform for thousands of adventures across the globe. However, as web technology evolved and the digital landscape shifted, the original platform was eventually retired.

The Reimplementation Project

This website is a reimplementation of Scout's original concept. The goal of this project is to keep the game alive for a new generation of GPS enthusiasts and to provide a home for the original community. While the backend and technology are new, the core gameplay mechanics remain faithful to Scout’s vision.

I hope to preserve the unique challenge and spirit of exploration that Scout created with Geodashing, and to provide a stable, community-driven platform for players to continue dashing for years to come.

Join the Dash

Whether you are a veteran dasher from the original GPSgames era or a newcomer discovering the challenge for the first time, we invite you to participate. Grab your GPS receiver or smartphone, find a dashpoint near you, and join the game.

Geodashing was originally created by Scout. This reimplementation is not affiliated with the original GPSgames.org but is dedicated to its memory.
