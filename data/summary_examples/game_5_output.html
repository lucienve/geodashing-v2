Results: Geodashing Game GD005 (Briars, Bogs, and Other Minor Inconveniences)


"Back on the grid! Indeed after mostly recovering from shoulder surgery, and now that I had a lengthy and uneventful Saturday planned, I was finally able to get out to this VERY local dashpoint. GD005-BIWG lies on the west side of Dover, NH... The Mrs. and I were able to drive up on Columbus and get a reading on the Geodashing site of being within 3 meters... Seriously: New Hampshire is known as 'The Granite State' for a reason - we have a LOT of rocks (almost as much cornfields as we had in Illinois). The people that settled this area just over 400 years ago moved a LOT of these large rocks from their farm fields to make boundary walls for property. The base stones of these rock walls were known as 'Two-Man' rocks, as they weighed between 90 and 213 KG, the 'Medium' or 'Stacking Stones' weigh anywhere from 23 to 90 KG, and the 'Capstones' weigh from 34-63 KG. These were stronger and sturdier folks than I."

That's Geodashing in New Hampshire with Markwell


"This is the first dashpoint I've visited in the new game, but not the first one ever. I submitted exactly one log in the original version. That must have been around 2010–2012, I don't remember exactly. I'm glad the game is back. This point sits in the middle of the Kellerwald-Edersee National Park, an old-growth beech forest of the kind that covered large parts of Central Europe for millennia. Patches of it survive across Europe, and the finest of them are now under strict protection. The park is one of five German sites in the UNESCO World Heritage listing of Europe's ancient beech forests. I combined the hunt with a 17 km (11 mi) hike through the beautiful forest on a sunny day."

That's Geodashing in Germany with jdonges

Game GD005 (Briars, Bogs, and Other Minor Inconveniences) of Geodashing ended in a tie for first place. The winners are DEEDEEDASH and douq millar with 15 points each!

Individual honors go to mapreader4 (9 points) and jdonges (6 points). Other dashers scoring points this month include Ash Doge (3 points), esem (3 points), lucienve (3 points), and Markwell (3 points).

The game saw 15 dashpoint hunts successfully logged across three countries (Germany, Poland, and the United States).

About Geodashing: Geodashing is a game in which players use GPS receivers on a playing field that covers the entire planet. The waypoints, or dashpoints, to be reached are randomly selected. The win goes to who can get to the most dashpoints; that is, if you can get to them at all! Each game has a new set of dashpoints making each game different and unpredictable. For more information and to play, visit Geodashing.org.
