Results: Geodashing Game GD003 (The Herd Questions Its Navigation Choices)


"GD003-AWZA lies next to Bear Creek Greenbelt Park in Lakewood, Colorado, so I took the opportunity to explore the park on my way to the dashpoint. I stopped at a bass pond shaped like a boomerang. From the jetty, I observed trios of largemouth bass swimming in tight vertical stacks, and the fishermen hoping to catch them. I then followed the paved Bear Creek trail, branching off onto the Sister City Park trail, which took me to the southern border of the park. From there, I headed across the grass until brought short by a fence about 25m from GD003-AWZA, which is on private property beneath a clump of bushes."

That's Geodashing in Colorado with Ash Doge


"Access to the DP is blocked by a gate at the bottom of a road into Eagle Ridge gated community just east of Watts Nar Lake about 8 miles south of Rockwood. TN. It was a pretty drive and a great start to the weekend even without reaching the DP. on the way back, we stopped at the Mt Roosevelt fire tower on the Mt Roosevelt Wildlife Management Are (WMA). The fire tower is no longer in use but the view from just below it is spectacular."

That's Geodashing in Tennessee with MsAdventure


"Thursday, 4 June, was going to be an after work excursion south towards Corona De Tucson. I needed a 'drive cycle' on my new catalytic converter in my trusty Tahoe Dashmobile before I took it back for a vehicle emissions retest. I was eastbound on Sahuarita Road then turned south onto E sycamore Springs Trail and was immediately thwarted by a gated community (for only 17 homes in the middle of nowhere). There were workers by the gate who were a bit surprised to see a vehicle turn in. I was only about 50 meters away from scoring! I waved as I turned around in the loop, took two snaps and then headed back north."

That's Geodashing in Arizona with Wisk


"Stopped here today on the way to Wayah Bald to hike the Appalachian and Bartram Trails. Right on the banks of the Tuckasegee River, on the edge of Webster, NC - a very small town of 372, located south of the better-known Sylva. A peaceful spot."

That's Geodashing in North Carolina with rhomicron

Game GD003 (The Herd Questions Its Navigation Choices) of Geodashing ended in a tie for first place. The winners are DEEDEEDASH and douq millar with 9 points each!

Other players who took to the field this month include lucienve (6 points), Ash Doge (3 points), MsAdventure (3 points), rhomicron (3 points), and Wisk (0 points).

The game saw 12 unique dashpoint hunts recorded across the United States, spanning the deserts of Arizona to the woods of Pennsylvania.

A sampling of waypoints visited by Geodashing players this month:

near Sahuarita, Arizona, where a gated community entrance with a tall saguaro cactus thwarted a player on an emissions-testing run ("immediately thwarted by a gated community (for only 17 homes in the middle of nowhere)").

on the edge of the Bear Creek Greenbelt Park in Lakewood, Colorado, where a player explored trails and stopped by a boomerang-shaped bass pond on a hot ninety-degree day.

near Fremont, Nebraska, in the driveway of a white house with a detached garage near the creeping suburbs of Wahoo.

near Goshen, Indiana, in an active field next to a utility pole from the Rural Electric Membership Corporation, where a player watched a self-propelled sprayer in the distance and spotted an Amish family in a horse-drawn buggy.

in a forested patch near Fremont, Nebraska, close to a cornfield where kids on ATVs were seen riding along a dusty gravel road.

along a creek near Fremont, Nebraska, where the air was thick with the smell of a nearby pig farm and the roadside fields were packed with tick-laden weeds.

on the tranquil banks of the Tuckasegee River in Webster, North Carolina, on a quiet drive toward an Appalachian Trail hike.

in a three-foot-tall cornfield on the outskirts of St. Edward, Nebraska, following a delicious lunch stop at Dusters.

near a gated community close to Watts Bar Lake in Oak Ridge, Tennessee, leading to a spectacular alternate stop at the historic Mount Roosevelt fire tower.

just outside a rod and gun club near Easton, Pennsylvania, on a winding forest road showing signs of centuries-old agricultural use.

just off the historic Memphis to Bristol Highway near Farragut, Tennessee, scorable from the road near a somewhat dodgy-looking storage yard.

Thanks to all the Geodashing players, whose many great reports are quoted here, not always with proper attribution. Complete, original reports are available on the Web site.

About Geodashing: Geodashing is a game in which players use GPS receivers on a playing field that covers the entire planet. The waypoints, or dashpoints, to be reached are randomly selected. The win goes to who can get to the most dashpoints; that is, if you can get to them at all! Each game has a new set of dashpoints making each game different and unpredictable. For more information and to play, visit Geodashing.org.
