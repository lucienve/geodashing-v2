This visit wasn't far from the busy Indiana Toll Road carrying much of the traffic between Chicago and the major cities of the East Coast, but a world away in experience.  I've driven this road many times over the last years, and the billboard advertising a Bill Engvall concert from October 26, 2018 has always been there.  Somewhat curious why it hasn't been replaced - it certainly gets lots of viewers.

This area is home to the largest Old Order Amish settlement in the US.  I passed a family in a horse-drawn buggy going down the 'E 600 N' grid road - I wondered what they'd make of my somewhat peculiarly modern random quest for the day.

The dashpoint itself was just off the road to the south, in an active field.  It was next to a wooden utility pole labeled 'Lagrance REMC 22247'.  The Rural Electric Membership Corporation brought power to many of these widely spread farms, following the Rural Electrification Act of 1936.

I spent a quiet 15 minutes here, watching the self-propelled sprayer off in the distance, along with the center-pivot irrigation system, and enjoying the solitude. Not a single car passed by.

In game 1, the nearest reachable dashpoint was 65 miles away. In game 2, it was closer, 35 miles. Finally in this game, a dashpoint landed in the Denver metro area.

GD003-AWZA lies next to Bear Creek Greenbelt Park in Lakewood, Colorado, so I took the opportunity to explore the park on my way to the dashpoint. I stopped at a bass pond shaped like a boomerang. From the jetty, I observed trios of largemouth bass swimming in tight vertical stacks, and the fishermen hoping to catch them.

I then followed the paved Bear Creek trail, branching off onto the Sister City Park trail, which took me to the southern border of the park. From there, I headed across the grass until brought short by a fence about 25m from GD003-AWZA, which is on private property beneath a clump of bushes.

It was the first 90-plus-degree day of the year, so I was glad to find a shaded trail alongside Bear Creek that took me back to my vehicle.
